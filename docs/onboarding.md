# Onboarding Guide (Atlas Freight, fictional sample company)

## Before day one

New hires receive their laptop by courier 3 days before their start date. The welcome email contains the first-login instructions and the name of their onboarding buddy.

## First week

Day one starts at 10:00 with a welcome session led by the People team. During the first week, each new hire completes the security awareness course and the data protection course; both are mandatory and take about 90 minutes in total.

Every newcomer is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 60 days. The buddy is a colleague from a different team who answers practical questions.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 4 months. A mid-probation review takes place after 8 weeks, and the final review happens two weeks before the end of probation.

## Engineering setup

Engineers get access to the source code on day two, after completing the security course. The first task is a small starter ticket labelled "good-first-issue", and the goal is to ship a first change to production within the first 10 working days.

Code changes need one approving review, or two for changes to the billing service. Deployments are frozen on Fridays after 14:00.

## Performance and feedback

Performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and December. One-to-one meetings with the line manager take place every two weeks and last 30 minutes.
