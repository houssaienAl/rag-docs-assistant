# Leave Policy (Atlas Freight, fictional sample company)

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2.25 days per month, starting from the first day of employment.

Up to 6 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be taken before 31 March, after which they are lost.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal called Compass. Requests of 5 consecutive days or more must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance. Shorter absences need 4 working days of notice. The line manager approves or declines within 2 working days.

## Sick leave

An employee who is ill must notify their manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of sickness. Sick days are paid at 100% of salary for the first 30 days per year.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 5 weeks, which can be split into two blocks during the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesday is the shared on-site day for all teams. Working from another country is allowed for a maximum of 20 working days per year and needs prior approval from HR.
