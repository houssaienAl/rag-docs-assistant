# IT Security Guidelines (Atlas Freight, fictional sample company)

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are stored in the company password manager, Vaultkeeper. Passwords are rotated only after a suspected compromise, not on a fixed schedule.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every account. Hardware security keys are required for administrators; other employees may use the authenticator app.

## Devices

Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within 10 days of release.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours so it can be wiped remotely. The helpdesk is reachable at extension 4410.

## Data classification

Company data has three levels: Public, Internal and Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer contracts and payroll, must never be sent by email and may only be stored in the encrypted document space called Strongroom.

## Phishing and incidents

Suspicious emails are reported with the "Report phish" button in the mail client. Never forward them to colleagues. Security incidents are handled by the on-call security engineer, who must acknowledge an alert within 15 minutes.

## Software

Only software listed in the internal catalogue may be installed. New tools are requested through an IT ticket and reviewed within 5 working days. Personal cloud storage accounts must not be used for company files.
