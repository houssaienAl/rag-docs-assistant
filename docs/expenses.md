# Expense and Travel Policy (Atlas Freight, fictional sample company)

## Submitting expenses

Expense claims are submitted in the finance tool called Ledgerly, with a photo of each receipt. Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the purchase. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll run, on the 28th of each month.

Any single expense above 400 euros requires written pre-approval from the department head.

## Meals

When travelling for work, meals are reimbursed up to 38 euros per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client dinners are capped at 70 euros per person, including the client.

## Travel

Train travel is the default for any journey under 4 hours and is booked in second class. Flights are booked in economy class; business class is only allowed for flights longer than 7 hours.

Hotels are reimbursed up to 140 euros per night in most cities, and up to 190 euros per night in Paris, London and Zurich.

## Equipment

Employees receive a home-office allowance of 350 euros, once every three years, for a desk, chair or monitor. Mobile phone plans are reimbursed up to 25 euros per month.

## Training

Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,200 euros for courses, certifications and conferences. Unused training budget does not carry over to the next year.
